Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

AI Innovators ("we," "us," "the app") teaches K-12 students about business and AI through interactive lessons, quizzes, and challenges. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how it's kept safe.

Who runs AI Innovators

AI Innovators is an independently-run educational project. If you have a question about this policy or your child's information, contact us at aiinnovatorsteam@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When someone creates an account, we collect:

  • A nickname the student chooses (we do not require a real full name)
  • An email address, used only to sign in and recover the account
  • A password, stored securely and encrypted by our authentication provider (Firebase Authentication, operated by Google) — we never see or store it in plain text

As a student uses the app, we also track:

  • Lesson and quiz progress, XP points, streaks, and badges earned
  • A chosen avatar/profile appearance

We do not collect a student's real name, birthdate, school, physical address, or location, and we do not ask for any of this information.

What we don't do

  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell or rent any information to third parties.
  • We do not use tracking for advertising purposes.
  • We do not allow students to message or contact each other through the app.

How information is used

We use account information only to let a student sign in, save their lesson progress across visits, and show their own achievements back to them. Progress data is used only to power features like streaks and badges — never shared outside the app.

Third-party services we use

We use Firebase (a Google service) to handle account sign-in and, if enabled, to send optional lesson reminder notifications. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

A note for parents

AI Innovators is built for students under 18, including children under 13. We designed the app to collect the minimum information needed for it to work: a nickname, an email, and a password — nothing more. Parents can request to view, correct, or delete their child's account information at any time by contacting us at the email above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
