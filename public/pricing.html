Questions parents ask

Nope — the Free plan needs no card at all. The 7-day trial on Premium/Family will ask for one, but you won't be charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel anytime before that.
🔁 Can I switch between monthly and yearly?
Yes, anytime from your account settings. Switching to yearly locks in the discounted rate right away.
❌ What happens if I cancel?
Your kid keeps access until the end of the billing period, then drops back to the Free plan — no progress is lost.
